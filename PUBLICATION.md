# Publication and data availability

Arzan.kz may be named as the source of the private production data. Retailer names,
aggregate findings, and aggregate visualizations may be published.

The production observations, product mappings, production-derived samples,
prediction rows, research databases, credentials, and private run artifacts must not
be redistributed. Public examples in this repository use deterministic synthetic
data only.

## Data availability statement

The production data supporting this study were provided by Arzan.kz and are subject
to third-party redistribution restrictions. Raw observations, product mappings, and
production-derived samples cannot be made public. Aggregate findings are reported in
the accompanying study materials. Analysis code and deterministic synthetic data
sufficient to exercise the research workflow are included in this repository. Access
to the production data may be requested directly from Arzan.kz and is subject to the
data owner's approval.
